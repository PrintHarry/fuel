The scale lies. The trend doesn't.

Daily weight jumps around by a kilo with water, salt and food in your gut. Fuel smooths every weigh-in into a trend line, so one bad morning never throws you off, and uses that trend to measure your real daily burn.

Daily scale weight Fuel's trend weight

Trend weight

Each weigh-in nudges the trend 10% of the way towards it. Noise cancels out; real change shows through.

Tn = Tn−1 + 0.1 × (Wn − Tn−1)
T = trend weight (kg), W = today's scale weight (kg). From The Hacker's Diet.

Your real burn

Every week Fuel compares what you ate with how your trend moved, then sets your target from the answer.

Burn = intake − 7,700 × Δtrend ÷ days
7,700 kcal ≈ 1 kg of body fat. Example: 2,487 + 7,700 × 0.2 ÷ 7 = 2,700 kcal a day.
